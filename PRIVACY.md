# NAVI Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

NAVI is an accessibility-focused Chrome extension for Google Sheets, Docs, and
Slides. It helps blind and visually impaired users understand and edit Google
Workspace content with text and voice commands.

## Information NAVI handles

NAVI may handle the following information when you use its features:

- The content and structure of the Google Sheet, Doc, or Slides presentation
  that you ask NAVI to read, explain, or edit.
- Your prompts, voice transcripts, and NAVI's responses.
- Microphone audio when you choose OpenAI Whisper for speech recognition.
- Response text when you choose OpenAI's natural voice.
- Your Google account email address when NAVI needs to explain an account or
  document access problem.
- Extension preferences, such as language, text size, speech settings, and up
  to 10 recent NAVI findings for the current browser session.

## How NAVI uses information

NAVI uses this information only to provide the features you request. This
includes reading and editing supported Google Workspace files, answering your
questions, remembering recent findings during the browser session, and
providing speech input and output.

NAVI does not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or use Google
Workspace data to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine
learning models.

## Where information is processed

- Google Workspace content is read or changed through Google APIs after you
  authorize access with your Google account.
- Prompts and relevant file content are sent to OpenAI to produce an answer.
- Microphone audio is sent to OpenAI only when Whisper speech recognition is
  selected.
- Response text is sent to OpenAI only when the natural OpenAI voice is
  selected.
- If browser speech recognition is selected, Chrome's speech service may
  process audio according to Google's and Chrome's settings and policies.

NAVI currently does not operate its own server or database. Google and OpenAI
may process and retain information according to their applicable terms and
privacy policies.

## Storage and retention

- Preferences are saved with Chrome synchronized storage and may follow your
  signed-in Chrome profile.
- Up to 10 recent NAVI findings are stored in Chrome session storage. They are
  removed when the browser session ends.
- NAVI does not intentionally retain Google Workspace content on a
  NAVI-operated server.

## Your choices

You can disable the wake word, choose browser speech instead of Whisper,
choose system speech instead of OpenAI's natural voice, or use the text input.
You can remove NAVI's Google access from your Google Account permissions and
can uninstall the extension at any time.

## Google API Limited Use disclosure

NAVI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Security

NAVI uses encrypted HTTPS connections when communicating with Google and
OpenAI. No API key should be embedded in a publicly distributed NAVI package.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when NAVI's features or data practices change. The
effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
